Paul asked about pricing this system for other clients (2026-07-21).

The system is generic enough to be rebranded for any PR, talent, or entertainment agency with minimal changes.

**Pricing discussed:**
- One-off setup fee for a single client: £5,000–£12,000 is defensible
- SaaS/subscription model: £150–£400/month per agency (small team)
- At £200/month × 10 clients = £24k/year recurring at near-zero running cost (Supabase + Vercel are free at this scale)

**Why:** The current build (directory + CRM) is strong but the roadmap features (Campaigns, Placements, Financial layer) would meaningfully increase the value proposition and justify higher pricing.

**How to apply:** If Paul mentions commercialising the app or pitching it to another agency, refer to this. Encourage him to wait until at least one of the roadmap modules is built before pitching — it makes the case for recurring fees much stronger.
